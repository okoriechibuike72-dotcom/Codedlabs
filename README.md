## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS

* **Total Customers:** 891
* **Total Value of Goods:** $942,360.55
* **Total Quantity of Goods:** 2,594

### 📦 Customer Order Cancellations

Possible reasons for customers cancelling orders include:

* **Slow delivery**

  * Delivery time may increase from one week to two weeks or longer.

* **Unexpected price changes**

  * Customers may encounter additional shipping costs or other unexpected charges.

* **Poor communication**

  * Customers may not receive:

    * Order confirmation
    * Tracking information
    * Timely responses to enquiries

### 📉 Decline in Prices in June

The sharp decline in prices observed in June may be associated with:

* Availability of substitute products.
* A decline in consumer demand.
* Increased competition from new market entrants.

### 💵 Cash as the Most Used Payment Method

The high use of cash payments may indicate:

* **Trust and security**

  * Customers may feel more comfortable using cash.

* **Convenience**

  * Cash may be readily available and easy to use.

* **Customer habits**

  * Customers may prefer payment methods they have traditionally used.

## 💼 RECOMMENDATIONS

* **Set expectations upfront**

  * Clearly communicate delivery timelines.
  * Provide order confirmation.
  * Provide customers with tracking information.

* **Communicate proactively**

  * Notify customers before them reaching out when there is a delivery delay.

* **Eliminate hidden charges**

  * Display the complete product price.
  * Clearly communicate shipping and delivery fees before checkout.

* **Monitor price trends**

  * Investigate significant monthly price changes.
  * Monitor customer demand and competitor activity.
  * Use these insights to support better pricing decisions.
