## INSIGHTS
-	There are 891 customers
-	The total price of the goods is $942,360.55
-	The total quantity of goods in the store is 2594
**	On the issue of a customer ordering a product and cancelling the order, the following could be the reasons:**
  -	Slow delivery (Delivery date changed from one week to two weeks)
  -	Prices changed after order (Hidden fees, shipping cost added later)
  - Poor communication (No order confirmation, no tracking, no response to enquiries)
**	The sharp decline in price noticed in June, could be as a result of:**
  - Substitute products in the market
  - Drop in consumer demand
  - New competitors enter the market
** The cash method as the most method of payment shows:**
	- Trust and security
  - Convenience
	- Habits (What they have used before
## RECOMMENDATION
- Set expectation upfront: clear delivery time, order confirmation and tracking link.
- Follow up fast: If there is a delay, message should be sent to the customer before he/she message the company
- The total price of the goods and the shipment fees should be made available (No hidden charges)
